Article 66 >- Obligation of Informing Occupational Risk Victim who sustained workplace accident, commuting accident or occupational diseases or victim representative shall inform promptly the National Social Security Fund, employer or employer representative about the occupational risk incurred except for force majeure. With formalities of informing and investigating the occupational risk and monitoring the compliance of employers and health facilities, the victim resulting from workplace accident, commuting accident or occupational diseases shall be regulated by Prakas of Minister of the Ministry in charge of social security schemes in accordance with the proposal of the NSSF Governing Body. Occupational disease list is regulated by Sub-Decree.
