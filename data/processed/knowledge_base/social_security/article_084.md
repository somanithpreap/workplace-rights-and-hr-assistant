Article 84 >- Activities related to Health and Society Activities related to health and society shall be established to complement each social security scheme as stipulated in this law. Activities involved with health and society have sources of fund as follows:

- Fine on employers related to the failure or guilty of complying with the law and other orders.

- Budget proposed for the activities in relation to health and society in each social security scheme.

The fund of activities involved with health and society shall be allocated as follows:

- Health prevention

- Occupational risk prevention

- Study, research and statistical survey of social security

- Education, dissemination and training

- Financial support or contribution with public or private institutions associated with the health and social activities for the benefits of the NSSF member.
