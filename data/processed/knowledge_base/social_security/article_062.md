Article 62 >- Revision of Occupational Risk Benefits Occupational risk benefits are pensions that shall be monitored and revised in accordance with consumer price index. Provider payment methods of Occupational Risk shall be in accordance with fee-for- service. Revision of the provider payment methods of Occupational Risk shall be regulated by Sub- Decree.
