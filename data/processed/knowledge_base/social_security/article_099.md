Article 99 >- Contribution Deduction in incompliance with the Provisions of the Law Any employer who has deducted the contributions of Occupational Risk from workers' wages or the contributions borne by himself by deducting from workers' wages for the contribution payment of Health Care, Pension or Unemployment Schemes shall be penalised for imprisonment of 1 (one) month to 1 (one) year and a fine of 120 (one hundred twenty) to 360 (three hundred sixty) times of daily base wage. This fine shall be applied equivalent to the number of workers from whom the employer has deducted contributions in the conditions in contrary to the provisions of this law.
