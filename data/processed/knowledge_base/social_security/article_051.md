Article 51 >- Funerary Grant Funerary grant shall be provided to the survivor of the deceased NSSF member due to non- work-related sickness or contingent accidents. Funerary grant shall be regulated by Prakas of the Minister in charge of social security schemes in accordance with the proposal of the NSSF Governing Body.
