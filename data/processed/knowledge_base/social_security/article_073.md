Article 73 >- Funerary Grant and Survivors' Pension The NSSF members as persons in public sector who sustain occupational risk leading to death, NSSF shall provide the funerary grant and survivors' pension. Funerary grant shall be provided to the beneficiaries under direct charge of victim or any person who takes responsibility for holding the funeral. The amount of funerary grant shall be regulated by Prakas of the Minister of the Ministry in charge of social security schemes in compliance with the proposal of the NSSF Governing Body. Survivors' pension shall be provided to the beneficiaries under the direct charge of victim. Calculation, formalities and procedures of providing the survivors' pension shall be regulated by Sub-Decree.
