Article 75 >- Medical Care Services Medical care services of Occupational Risk for Persons Defined by the Provisions of the Labour Law including personnel serving in air and maritime transportation as well as domestic workers shall be applicable for the same as the one for persons in public sector as stipulated in article 69 of this law.
