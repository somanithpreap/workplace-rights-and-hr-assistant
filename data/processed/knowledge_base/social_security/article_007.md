Article 7 >- Occupational Risk Scheme Social Security Scheme on Occupational Risk for Persons under Public Sector shall be a compulsory contribution that State shall solely pay contributions of Occupational Risk. Social Security Scheme on Occupational Risk for Persons Defined by the Provisions of the Labour Law including personnel serving in the air and maritime transportation as well as domestic workers shall be a compulsory contribution that employers shall have a compulsory to pay contributions of Occupational Risk.
