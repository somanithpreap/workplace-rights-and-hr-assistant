Article 58 >- Occupational Risk Benefits Occupational risk benefits consist of medical care benefit, daily allowance for temporary disability, pension for permanent disability, allowance for permanent disability, survivors' pension, funerary grant and rehabilitation services. For the intentionally caused accident, the victim shall not have entitlement to enjoy occupational risk benefits.
