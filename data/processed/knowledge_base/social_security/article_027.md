Article 27 >- Invalidity Pension The NSSF members as persons in public sector fall into the invalidity shall be entitled to enjoy the invalidity pension only if the persons concerned fulfil the following conditions:

1. Have registered in Pension Scheme

2. Have paid contributions of Pension Scheme for at least 60 (sixty) months before the date of incurring the invalidity.
