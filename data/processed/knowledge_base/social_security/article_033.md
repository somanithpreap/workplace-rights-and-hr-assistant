Article 33 >- Survivors' Pension Beneficiaries of survivors' pension shall be entitled to enjoy survivors' pension only if the persons concerned who are old-age pensioner, invalidity pensioner or the NSSF members have paid contributions of Pension Scheme for at least 60 (sixty) months before death. The minimum rate of Survivors' Pension shall be equivalent to 45 (forty-five) percent of old-age pensions or invalidity pensions of the person concerned. Survivors' Pension is divided as follows:

- Spouse: 50 (fifty) percent

- Child : 50 (fifty) percent

- In case the beneficiary is only spouse or children shall be entitled to enjoy 100 (one hundred) percent.
