Article 92 >- Establishment of Coordination Mechanism Coordination mechanism shall be established between each social security scheme for persons under public sector, persons defined by the provisions of the Labour Law including personnel serving in air and maritime transportation as well as domestic workers, the self-employed and persons who are not covered by this law. NSSF shall sign the agreement with the social security institutions of the countries of destination with a view to establishing the protection mechanism of social security for migrant workers and in line with formalities and procedures coming into force. Formalities of the above-mentioned coordination mechanism implementation shall be regulated by Sub-Decree.
