Article 37 >- Conditions, Formalities, and Procedures of Implementation Persons in public sector can contribute to Voluntary Pension Scheme. Mechanism for determining contribution rate of Voluntary Pension Scheme is applied the same as the one of Mandatory Pension Scheme. Conditions, formalities and procedures of the voluntary contribution of persons in public sector are regulated by Sub-Decree.
