Article 65 >- Service Quality Monitoring of Health Facility Recognition of medical care service quality shall be compiled with the legal instruments issued by Ministry of Health. The service quality of health facility signed the agreement with NSSF shall be monitored and evaluated by the Social Security Medical Council.
