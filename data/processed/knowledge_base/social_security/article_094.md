Article 94 >- Prerogative of Collection of Contribution, Debt and Asset NSSF shall have the prerogative of the public power for collecting contribution, having debt paid and protecting assets. NSSF shall have the privilege of all assets of its debtors in the first order after the debt is paid to workers. Procedures of the prerogative implementation and the debt collection of NSSF shall be regulated by Sub-Decree.
