Article 70 >- Daily Allowance for Temporary Disability In the period of treating any injuries or diseases resulting from occupational risk in the health facilities and the convalescent care prescribed by the medical doctor or physician recognized by NSSF, the salary of the victim concerned shall be provided as usual.
