Article 98 >- Objection to Contribution Payment Employers of enterprises/establishments who do not comply with the contribution payment by the deadline or submit the incorrect number of workers as stipulated in article 82 of this law shall be liable to a fine of 10 (ten) to 30 (thirty) times of daily base wage. This fine shall be applied equivalent to the number of workers are employed in the conditions in contrary to the provisions of this law.
