Article 19 >- Pension Contribution Contribution rate of Pension Scheme for Persons Defined by the Provisions of the Labour Law including personnel serving in air and maritime transportation as well as domestic workers is regulated by Sub-Decree. Contribution rate of Pension Scheme for the self-employed is regulated by Sub-Decree. Contribution rate of Pension Scheme shall be raised up by the scaled premium method or the suitable method approved by the National Social Protection Council.
