Article 43 >- Revision of Health Care Benefits Health care benefits shall be revised based on the actual health situation. Provider payment methods shall be in compliance with case-based mechanism and/or fee- for-service mechanism. The revision of provider payment methods for Health Care Scheme by other methods shall be regulated by Sub-Decree.
