Article 103 >- Grandfathering Pension The NSSF members as persons defined by the provisions of the Labour Law including personnel serving in air and maritime transportation as well as domestic workers whose age are over 30 (thirty) years as from the date of launching Pension Scheme shall have entitlement to receive the Grandfathering Pension. The Grandfathering Pension shall be provided only if person concerned has paid contributions for at least 24 (twenty-four) months within 36 (thirty-six) months as from the date of launching Pension Scheme.
