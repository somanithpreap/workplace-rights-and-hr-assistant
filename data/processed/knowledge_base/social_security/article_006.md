Article 6 >- Health Care Scheme Social Security Scheme on Health Care for Persons under Public Sector shall be a compulsory contribution that State and persons in public sector, former civil servants and veterans shall have a compulsory to pay contributions together. Social Security Scheme on Health Care for Persons Defined by the Provisions of the Labour Law including personnel serving in the air and maritime transportation as well as domestic workers shall be a compulsory contribution that employer and persons defined by the provisions of the labour law including personnel serving in the air and maritime transportation as well as domestic workers shall have a compulsory to pay contributions together. Social Security Scheme on Health Care for the self-employed is regulated by Sub-Decree.
