3. Content of Prakas 443 on Payment of Seniority Indemnity

A. Payment of seniority indemnity from 2019 (Article 2 of the Prakas) Date of implementation  From 2019 onwards Payment  Equal to 15 days of wage and other benefits per year  Make two payments per year for workers/employees who are working:

- 7.5 days in June of each year and

- 7.5 days in December of each year  For the first year of employment, workers/employees who have seniority from 1 month to 6 months shall receive 7.5 days of seniority indemnity.  Have no maximum (provide forever for the rest of the job's life.
