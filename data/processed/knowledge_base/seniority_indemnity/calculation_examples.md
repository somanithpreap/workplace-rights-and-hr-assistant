For example: An enterprise/establishment employs 110 workers/employees starting in different years Staff Number of Year of Seniority before 2019 Total seniority indemnity Type staff (persons) joining (counting as of the end of (15 days/year) 2018) 1 20 2008 11 years * 156 days (15 x 10) + 6 2 30 2012 7 years 105 days (15 x 7) 3 40 2016 3 years 45 days (15 x 3) 4 10 2018 1 year 15 days (15 x 1) 5 10 2019 0 year 0

* For staff type 1 who has seniority of 11 years receive the total seniority indemnity of 156 days (26 days of working days per month x 6 months which is the maximum), not 165 days. To find the number of working days per month, take 52 which is the number of weeks per year to be multiplied by 6 which is the number of working days per week (so, the number of working days per year is 52 x 6 = 312). Then take 312 which is the number of working days per year to be divided by 12 which is the number of months per year. So the number of working days per month is 312/12 = 26.
