B. Requirement for payment of seniority that exists before 2019

Article Two of the Law on Amendment

"The calculation and payment of seniority of workers/employees who have worked before this law takes effect shall follow the formality determined by a Prakas of the Minister in charge of labour."

 Purpose: The purpose of this Article 2 is to protect the interests of workers/employees who are protected by the Labour Law 1997. If it does not stipulate so, workers/employees who have seniority before 2019, especially workers/employees who have many years of seniority will lose their benefit due to the amendment to the Labour Law.
