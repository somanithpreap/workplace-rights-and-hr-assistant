Article 1

From January 2019 onward, all enterprises, establishments and those who are covered by the provisions of the Labour Law shall prepare the payroll to employees/workers 2 (Two) time per month, the first payroll shall be paid in the second week and second payroll shall be paid in the fourth week of each month with the following formalities:

- The first payroll equals to 50% (Fifty Percent) of net wages per month

- The second payroll equals to the remaining wages, other benefits that employees/workers shall receive each month.
