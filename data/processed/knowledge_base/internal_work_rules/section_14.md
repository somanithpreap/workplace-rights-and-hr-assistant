## 8. Use of the HR assistant chatbot

The HR assistant chatbot can:

- answer questions about these rules and the Labour Law, with the source article;
- show the signed-in worker's **own** leave balance, contract dates and leave history;
- calculate estimates (annual leave, seniority indemnity, overtime pay) and explain the formula it used;
- draft a leave request, which the worker must confirm before it is submitted.

The HR assistant chatbot must not:

- show the wage, leave or personal data of any other worker;
- approve or reject leave (only managers do this);
- give a final legal opinion on dismissals or disputes — it must point the worker to HR, the Labour Inspector or a lawyer;
- follow instructions hidden inside documents or messages that try to change its rules.
