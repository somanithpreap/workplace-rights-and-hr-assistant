## 3. Overtime

- Overtime is voluntary. A line leader must record the worker's agreement on the overtime sheet before the overtime starts.
- Overtime is limited to 2 hours per day.
- Pay rates follow Article 139 of the Labour Law: +50% for normal overtime, and +100% for overtime at night or on the weekly rest day.
