## 2. Working hours and shifts

- Normal working time is 8 hours per day and 48 hours per week, Monday to Saturday.
- Two day shifts are used: **Day A** 07:00–16:00 and **Day B** 08:00–17:00, each with a one-hour lunch break that is not counted as working time.
- Sunday is the weekly rest day.
- Arriving more than 15 minutes late three times in one calendar month leads to a written warning.
