## 7. Grievances

1. Talk to the line leader or the shop steward.
2. If not solved within 5 working days, submit a written grievance to HR.
3. If still not solved, the worker may contact the Labour Inspector of the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training. Collective disputes may go to the Arbitration Council.

Retaliation against a worker for raising a grievance is forbidden.
