## 4. Pay

- Wages are paid twice a month by bank transfer, as required by Prakas No. 442 (2018): the first payment (50% of the net monthly wage) in the second week of the month, and the balance with other benefits in the fourth week.
- The 2026 minimum wage for the garment sector (USD 210 per month) is the floor for all production workers who have completed probation.
- Attendance bonus: USD 10 per month for workers with no unauthorised absence and no more than one late arrival in the month.
- Seniority indemnity is paid in **June** and **December** each year, following Prakas No. 443 of the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training (see the seniority indemnity guidance document).
