### 5.1 Annual leave
- Annual leave follows Articles 166–167 of the Labour Law.
- Requests for annual leave must be submitted **at least 3 working days** before the first day of leave, through the HR assistant chatbot or the HR office.
- During the peak production months (**August and September**), annual leave of more than 3 consecutive working days needs the approval of the Production Manager.
