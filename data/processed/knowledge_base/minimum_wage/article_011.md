Article 11.-

The Minister of the Ministry in Charge of Labour shall issue Prakas on the schedule of minimum wage fixing after having received recommendation from the National Minimum Wage Council.

In the event of force majeure or a necessity, the Minister in Charge of Labour may issue Prakas to suspend or adjust the schedule for discussion of the minimum wage. In this case, the previous year's minimum wage shall remain valid until after a new minimum wage is set and supersedes.
