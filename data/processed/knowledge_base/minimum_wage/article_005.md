Article 5.-

The elements to be considered in defining the minimum wage shall focus on the economic condition, cost of living and prevailing situation of the country. Those elements are:

1. Social criteria including family status, inflation rate, and cost of living.

2. Economic criteria including productivity, competitiveness of the country, labour market situation and profitability of the sector.

In case of necessity the National Minimum Wage Council can adjust the minimum wage fixing criteria in accordance to economic activities, profession, jobs or regions based on economic and social conditions of the country.
