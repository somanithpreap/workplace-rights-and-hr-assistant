Article 6.-

Any written or verbal agreement that falls within the scope of the minimum wage and would remunerate the worker at a rate less than the minimum wage shall be null and void.

Any written or verbal agreement that falls within the scope of the minimum wage and would remunerate the worker at a rate higher than the minimum wage shall be continued.
