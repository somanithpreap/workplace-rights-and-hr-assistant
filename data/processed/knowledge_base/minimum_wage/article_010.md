Article 10.-

The discussion of minimum wage shall conform to the following principles:

1. The discussion of minimum wage shall take place per the schedule set by the Minister of the Ministry in Charge of Labour.

2. The minimum wage shall be the predictable figure or increment rate that grows gradually steady.

3. The win-win policy shall be observed.

4. The use of official and legal data of the national institutions or entities in charge of compiling statistics, and social and economic criteria as stated in Article 5 of this law shall serves as the basis of the discussion.
