Article 24.-

The fine of not more than 40,000,000 (twenty million) riel shall be imposed to any employer who violates the provisions of articles 4, 6, 7 or 8 of this law.

In addition to the above punishment, the employer who pays the worker at a rate less than the minimum wage shall repay the violated amount to the worker taking into account inclusion of the interest earned at a rate set by laws.
